How I think about it

The unlock isn't use cases. It's leverage.

Most people get stuck brainstorming what to ask AI. The real shift is what AI can connect to and act on. Four pieces turn a chatbot into something that does work for you.

Leverage = Context + Integrations + Tools + Memory
1

Context

It knows your goals, your voice, and how you actually work.

2

Integrations

It reaches your real tools: email, calendar, files, the systems you live in.

3

Tools

It can take action (draft, schedule, summarize, fetch), not just talk about it.

4

Memory

It remembers what matters across days, so it gets sharper the longer you use it.
